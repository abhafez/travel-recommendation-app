About Wanderlust

Wanderlust was founded in 2020 by a small team of travel enthusiasts who believed that finding the perfect destination shouldn't be complicated. What started as a shared spreadsheet of our favorite beaches, temples, and hidden gems has grown into a platform used by thousands of travelers worldwide.

Our mission is simple: connect people with unforgettable places through curated, honest recommendations — no sponsored fluff, just destinations we'd genuinely visit ourselves.
